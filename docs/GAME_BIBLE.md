# NITK: Fresher Year: game bible

The game is *Bully*'s structure (one school year, cliques, chapters, a campus you learn by heart) set at NITK Surathkal. Nobody gets hurt and nobody gets ragged. The conflict comes from ordinary college pressure: attendance, CGPA, the mess, club recruitments, fests, the monsoon, and the sunset you promised to catch.

Facts come from web research (sources at the end). Anything marked **[confirm]** comes from a single source or is my inference; check it with people who lived it.

---

## 1. Design rules (the "safe Bully" contract)

Everything *Bully* resolved with fists, this game resolves with a challenge:

| *Bully* had | NITK game has |
|---|---|
| Fist fights | Debates (LSD), code duels (IE Code / WebClub), Robowars (bots fight, not people), sports matches, dance-offs (Step Up, Tandav), quizzes |
| Pranks on rivals | Harmless inter-hostel rivalry: banners, Crescendo points, Freshers Cup trash talk |
| Detention / prefects | Attendance shortage, warden notices, library fines, a missed mess slot |
| Bullying the weak | **Never.** Seniors show up as mentors, club heads and recruiters. Ragging is illegal in Indian colleges; the game has zero ragging content, and "report to the anti-ragging committee" can appear as a system message |
| Money from mischief | Money from a TA-ship, a hackathon prize, an E-Pitch win, a part-time photography gig at Incident |

No weapons, no alcohol, no drugs. Romance, if any, is "invite someone to watch the sunset at the lighthouse", nothing more.

## 2. Player stats

| Stat | Real anchor | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| **CGPA** | NITK grades on a 10-point scale: O=10, A+=9, A=8, B+=7, B=6, C=5, P=4, F=0. Internal assessment is ~30–40%, the end-sem exam ~60–70%. CGPA×10 ≈ percentage | Class minigames and exams move SGPA; CGPA gates some missions (e.g. TA-ship, exclusive club interviews) |
| **Attendance** | 75% is the norm at Indian institutes **[confirm NITK rule]** | Each class you skip costs attendance; dropping below 75% triggers an "attendance shortage" arc |
| **Energy** | – | Drains while running and with late nights; sleep in your hostel room |
| **Hunger** | Mess, night canteens, Nescafe, Nandini | Food buffs (see §6) |
| **Wallet (₹)** | – | Maggi, xerox, fest passes, bus to Mangalore |
| **Rep** (per faction) | – | Opens missions, discounts, club invites |
| **Year** | Exclusive clubs are off-limits to first years | Progression across years (§8) |

Freshers' mess choice is real and makes a good first decision: **1st block veg, 2nd block non-veg, or limited seats at 7th block veg.**

### Clubs: the cliques you join
- **Exclusive clubs (locked in year 1):** IEEE, ACM, Rotaract, ISTE, IE (Institution of Engineers), IET. First-years are turned away ("come back next year"), which is the game's built-in progression hook.
  - **IE SIGs:**
    - **Code**: competitive coding
    - **Gadget**: "heavily doped" electronics people
    - **Garage**: the oldest SIG, gets its hands dirty
    - **Robotics**: all SIGs together, bot building
    - **Script**: discussion and ideas
  - **IEEE groups:** Computer Society, Signal Processing, Circuits & Systems, **Piston**, Women in Technology, SIGHT (humanitarian tech).
- **Non-exclusive (open to freshers):**
  - **WebClub** (Web Enthusiasts' Club), described as the de-facto CS club
  - Robotics Club
  - Linux Users Group
  - Star Gazing Club
  - Photography
  - Music, Dance, Drama
  - Yoga, Art
  - **LSD**: Literary, Stage and Debating Society
  - **SPICMACAY** (NITK hosts one of the biggest chapters in the country)
  - **E-FOREA**: entrepreneurship, runs **E-Pitch**
  - NSS, NCC
- **Project teams:**
  - **NITK Racing**: Formula Student team since 2010, electric and combustion cars, **won Formula Bharat 2021**
  - **IRIS**: the student-built portal the whole institute runs on
- **Committees:**
  - **Phoenix**: hostel sports (Freshers Cup, floodlight tournament, Olympia athletics, Coliseum inter-branch fest)
  - **Crescendo**: hostel culture. Its logo's five colours are Dance, Music, Literature, Drama and Sports.
- **Media:**
  - **Pulse**: student publication, fresher FAQs
  - **The Lighthouse**: independent campus-issues publication

## 5. Campaign: one fresher year, five chapters

The calendar is real: the odd semester runs July/Aug–Dec with **Engineer in October**; the even semester runs Jan–May with **Incident in March**.

### Chapter 1: "Srinivasnagar" (August, monsoon): **built**
*Built as six missions (Main Gate, Three Messes, Wheels, Log in to IRIS, Maggi in the Rain, The Sunset Rule) in `src/game/chapter1.ts`. The design below was the plan; the README lists what shipped.*
*The campus is named Srinivasnagar after founder U. Srinivas Mallya.*

1. **Main Gate.** Get off the bus on NH66, cross by the overbridge, and find the Main Building's academic section. *Tutorial: movement, map, teleport.*
2. **Room Key.** Find Karavali (Block 1) and meet your roommate NPC.
3. **Three Messes.** Choose 1st block veg, 2nd block non-veg, or 7th block veg (limited seats: a timed race). The choice shapes the whole year's food buffs.
4. **Log In to IRIS.** Register courses on the student-built portal; a puzzle minigame at the computer centre.
5. **Maggi in the Rain.** It's pouring (the coast gets >3000 mm a year). Get from LHC to Nescafe before it closes, dry-ish. *Umbrella physics, puddles, a rain-weather system.*
6. **The Sunset Rule.** A senior tells you everyone goes to the beach at sunset. Reach lighthouse hill before the sun touches the sea, and avoid the bees.

### Chapter 2: "Recruitments" (September): **built**
*Built in `src/game/chapter2.ts` as Recruitment Week, Come Back Next Year, Freshers Cup (penalty shootout), Flat Tyre (a chase with an Aravali prankster), sudo make me a coffee (Linux terminal), and First Light (constellations). The Engineer chapter is next.*
7. **Stall Crawl.** Club recruitment week: visit the non-exclusive stalls and pick up to three.
8. **Come Back Next Year.** Try the exclusive clubs. IEEE, ACM, IE, ISTE, IET and Rotaract each politely turn you away, and each one leaves an entry in your "Next Year" journal.
9. **Freshers Cup** (Phoenix). A football or cricket match on Main Ground for your block.
10. **Linux Install Fest.** Help the Linux Users Group revive a hostel-mate's laptop.
11. **First Light** (Star Gazing Club). A clear night, the telescope on the Main Ground, and five constellations to find. It only triggers on a clear night.

### Chapter 3: "Engineer" (October)
*Engineer began in its current form in 2005. It now runs three to four days, has 28 events across five tracks, and draws 5000+ students from 60+ colleges.*

12. **Arena Build.** Robowars needs its arena up. Ferry parts from the workshop, then assemble it (a construction minigame).
13. **Robowars.** Drive your club's bot in the arena. Bots take damage; people don't.
14. **Overnight Hackathon.** Code-duel minigame against rival teams; energy management through the night.
15. **Hospitality Desk.** Guide lost visiting students to venues across both sides of NH66 (timed escort/delivery missions).
16. **Push Start.** NITK Racing needs volunteers to push the car to the test run, then help find a telemetry bug.

### Chapter 4: "Midsems to Endsems" (November–December)
17. **Seat Hunt.** The library at exam time: find a free seat before it fills (a stealth-ish silence minigame).
18. **Xerox Run.** Collect a friend's notes, get them photocopied in town, and be back before the night canteen closes.
19. **Attendance Shortage.** If you dropped under 75%, talk your way through to the professor, with a real consequence either way. Otherwise it's a bonus mission helping a friend catch up.
20. **Endsem Week.** Five exam minigames matched to your branch; the results set your SGPA.

### Chapter 5: "Crescendo & Incident" (January–March)
*Incident started on 17 Sept 1980, founded by Umar Teekay on ₹10,000. Its first theme came in 1997: "Fusion Confusion".*

21. **Five Colours.** Crescendo inter-hostel: Dance, Music, Literature, Drama, Sports. Represent your block in at least three.
22. **Floor It.** An LSD debate at SJA, with a dialogue-choice system for arguments. The best comeback wins.
23. **Step Up.** Dance battle (a rhythm minigame), then solo, then duet, then Tandav, then Promenade.
24. **Sandemonium.** Incident beach day: volleyball, beach football and sand events.
25. **Pro-Night Crew.** Stage logistics at SAC: cables, lights, a crowd-flow puzzle. The rain threatens the show.
26. **Finale: Sunset Together.** Round up every friend you made across the factions and get them to the lighthouse hill before the sun sets. Credits roll over the sea.

## 6. Side activities and collectibles

- **Food quests:** goli baje and neer dosa in Surathkal, Nandini's Oreo shake, Atithi's Chinese, and the monthly grand dinner. Each gives a buff (e.g. Maggi = +energy in rain).
- **SPICMACAY evenings** at SJA: a listening minigame.
- **Coastal clean-up** (Rotaract/NSS): sweep a stretch of NITK Beach. Blood donation camp.
- **E-Pitch** (E-FOREA): pitch a hostel startup idea for prize money.
- **Photo Hunt** (Photography Club): capture landmarks in camera mode at the right time of day (lighthouse at sunset, Main Building at night).
- **Kannada / Tulu phrasebook:** talk to town NPCs. Borrows SADAK's phrasebook mechanic (and could use its Sarvam voice layer).
- **Lore cards** hidden around campus:
  - Foundation stone laid 6 Aug 1960 as KREC
  - Became NITK in 2002
  - U. Srinivas Mallya and "Srinivasnagar"
  - The lighthouse: tower built 1969–72; BBT Paris optics lit on 15 May 1972; 41 m, red and white
  - Incident's 1980 founding and ₹10,000 budget
  - Engineer's 2005 relaunch
  - NITK Racing's Formula Bharat 2021 win
  - IRIS, the student-built portal
  - Notable alumni (K. V. Kamath, Nandini Shenoy, and others)

## 7. World tie-ins already in the engine

- Buildings, grounds, the beach, the lighthouse and NH66 come from OpenStreetMap, so mission triggers can key off OSM names. That's the same mechanism landmarks use (`src/world/landmarks.ts`).
- Time of day exists (morning, noon, sunset, night). Missions like **The Sunset Rule**, **First Light** and **Sunset Together** can check it directly.
- Teleport and places search exist and become the fast-travel system.
- Still needed:
  - a mission system: data-driven missions, triggers, objectives, a HUD tracker
  - NPCs: SADAK's crowd and people code can be ported
  - dialogue: SADAK's LLM-driven NPC dialogue could power seniors, profs and club heads
  - weather: rain
  - the minigames

## 8. Years 2–4 (expansion hooks)

- **Year 2:** exclusive clubs open; the "Next Year" journal becomes a quest list; IE SIG projects.
- **Year 3:** move into a Mega Tower (Everest, Himalaya or Kailash). Internship season. Become the recruiter at the stall you were turned away from.
- **Year 4:** final-year project, placements, running Engineer or Incident as a core-team member, and a graduation walk out through the Main Gate.
